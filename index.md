<!-- # Rafael Redondo Tejedor -->

{: style="font-size:16px;" align="justify"}
I am **Director of the [Multimedia Technologies Unit at Eurecat](https://eurecat.org/en/field-of-knowledge/multimedia-technologies/)** since **2026**, leading R&D in AI, computer vision, multimedia and interaction. With more than two decades of experience across academic research and applied innovation, my work focuses on translating advances in **deep learning, multimodal systems and generative AI** into technologies with scientific and industrial impact.

{: style="font-size:16px;" align="justify"}
My research career evolved from **biologically inspired vision models** for **multiscale image fusion and compression** during my **PhD and postdoctoral work at** [**IO-CSIC**](https://www.csic.es/es/el-csic/organizacion/institutos-centros-y-unidades/instituto-de-optica-daza-de-valdes) and [**ETSIT–UPM**](https://www.etsit.upm.es/) (**2002–2010**), to **biomedical and microscopy imaging** at [**VISILAB–UCLM**](https://www.uclm.es/ciudad-real/etsii/etsii-cr/investigacion/vislab) (**2010–2015**). After a pivotal **MSc in Sonology** at **[UPF](https://www.upf.edu/)–[ESMUC](https://www.esmuc.cat/es/)** (**2012**) and joining [**Eurecat**](https://eurecat.org/) in Barcelona (**2015**), I moved progressively toward **interactive technologies and applied deep learning** in several topics such as face analysis or 360° depth estimation, and later, as **Head of the Image Group (2022–2025)**, toward **multimodal and generative AI**, including sound-to-video generation, diffusion models for medical and satellite imagery, neural rendering, 3D reconstruction and avatars. My work includes more than **40 publications in international journals and conferences**, including IJCV, ECCV, ICCV and ICIP.

{: style="font-size:16px;" align="justify"}
A central part of my career in the last decade is built on translating foundational research into **impactful industrial solutions across sectors including ICT, cultural and creative industries, retail, mobility, manufacturing, robotics, and health**. I have led and contribute to collaborative [**European and Spanish R&D projects**](https://multimedia-eurecat.github.io/projects/) that connect research with industrial challenges and technology transfer. These include the Horizon Europe initiatives **AUTOPOST, PHASE IV AI** and **INDUX-R**, the Spanish AI network of excellence [**AI4ES**](https://www.ai4es.com), Catalan government-funded projects such as **ViVIM** and **Mironins**, and several [**CIDAI Impact Projects**](https://cidai.eu/es/proyectos-cidai/proyectos-de-impacto/). I have also served as an evaluator for [**The Collider**](https://mobileworldcapital.com/en/the-collider/), contributing to the promotion of research-driven technology and innovation ventures.

{: style="font-size:16px;" align="justify"}
I combine scientific and technological leadership with a long-standing interest at the intersection of **image, sound, and interactive technologies**. This interdisciplinary perspective continues to shape both my research and my exploration of new forms of audiovisual and creative computing. For more, visit my [blog](/blog), [creative projects](https://valillon.art) or [code](https://github.com/valillon).
